# Care Homes Finance research

Self-published research by Matt Lenzie / Care Homes Finance. Contact: enquiries@carehomesfinance.co.uk.

## Age of outstanding bridging charges, October 2026

[Care Home Finance 2026: Bridging Charges More Than Two Years Old](https://anyflip.com/solvf/cfdv/)

In the 1 October 2026 snapshot, 110 of 204 outstanding charges in the selected bridging and short-term lender subset were more than 24 months old (53.9%). This two-page note separates the overlapping source counts into three age bands and explains why a registered charge does not establish unpaid debt or an overdue loan. Known term lenders are excluded. It uses the same source programme and authorship as the reports below. This is a stock of registered charges at one date; the lender-activity report below measures a flow of financing events over January to August. Their units and periods must not be combined.

## Regional activity in the first half of 2026

[Care Home Finance 2026: Where Activity Is Rising and Falling](https://www.calameo.com/books/00829566942a230cdfeb3)

The three-page regional note compares H1 2026 with H1 2025. Four reporting regions rose and three fell. Midlands increased from 133 to 146 financing events; East of England fell from 64 to 53. Its chart, table and methods explain why overlapping regional counts must not be added to the separate national total of 552 events. The report is publicly readable on Calameo and uses the same source programme and authorship as the other notes here. The lender comparison below covers the later January to August period.

## Lender activity in 2026

[Care Home Finance 2026: Lender Activity](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/constructioncapital/care-home-finance-2026-lender-activity)

Challenger and specialist banks recorded 117 financing events in January to August 2026, compared with 81 in the same months of 2025. The report and calculation package show how changes across lender groups add up to the national increase of 28 events. It includes the three-page report, source counts, calculated tables, Python code and a starter notebook. The public data card can be read without signing in; Kaggle requires sign-in for its archive download.

This uses the same [Care Home Finance Report 2026](https://carehomesfinance.co.uk/reports/care-home-finance-2026/) source programme as the counting-method note below. Event counts approximate finance arrangements and do not measure amounts lent.

## The leading lender's growing share

[Care Home Finance: The Leading Lender's Growing Share, 2023 to 2025](https://zenodo.org/records/23913583) | [Permanent DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23913583)

The leading bank or specialist lender accounted for 15.2% of recorded financing events in 2023 and 24.7% in 2025. The top five lenders' combined share changed little. This report explains the figures and what they can tell care-home operators. A financing event is a group of security filings that may relate to one finance arrangement; it does not measure the amount lent. Both notes are by Matt Lenzie for Care Homes Finance and use the same source data, so they are not separate surveys.
